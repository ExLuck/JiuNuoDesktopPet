# Two-pet interactions

Status: the three scripted interactions, the "two-pet relay for deleted icons" and the four new [daily interactions](COMPANION_LIFE.md) are all wired up. Version, verification and release evidence are kept together in [current progress](CHARACTERS.md#current-progress-and-phase-exit-conditions), and how the pet windows actually look is for the user to test.

## Adopted behaviour

While "Both pets together" is on, idling, curiosity and ordinary wandering, exploring and patrolling can all accept a two-pet invitation. Apart from feeding, both have to be on the same monitor and move into the interaction positions first; feeding keeps them where they are and may cross monitors. Dragging, cheek pinching, eating, climbing and window attacks are never interrupted by an invitation, and a sleeping character accepts only the nap invitation. A window already stored in Feibi's hat does not block an interaction; it stays in its own storage slot, the existing ownership safety checks continue to run during the interaction, and nothing is returned automatically. Clicking a character, deliberately asking for mischief or for a window back, turning coexistence off, going home or quitting all clean up the scene. Each character's life state is saved separately, and a scene never modifies real files.

1. **Cheek pinch**: Feibi Jiubi approaches from the right and reaches out for a gentle pinch, and Nuonuo's cheek stretches to the right then springs softly back. The performance is about 2.7 seconds, with the approach timed separately.
2. **Napping together**: the two lean towards each other, yawn, sit down and rest their heads together; after falling asleep a breathing loop runs, ending about 18.2 seconds after they are in place, and sleeping genuinely reduces both characters' sleepiness. In quiet mode this is the only interaction allowed.
3. **Hat-off food throwing from where they stand** (2026-09-29): no distance threshold, no moving together, and height differences and separate monitors are allowed. Both turn towards each other where they stand, Feibi takes her hat fully off, pulls out a snack and throws it at 3.86 seconds, then puts the hat back on. The physical-pixel hand and mouth anchors include the facing, body size and DPI transforms, and the food stays on its own transparent layer. Flight time adjusts with distance between 0.7 and 4 seconds, the parabola connects different heights, frame 9 of Nuonuo's mouth opening is aligned with the arrival moment, and she carries on chewing afterwards rather than finishing early just because the distance was long. The snack is an apple, cake, doughnut or pudding; on a real catch, hunger drops by 9 in normal mode, while fasting and infinite modes leave hunger unchanged. It aborts if the window they are standing on is moved, hidden or minimized, rather than letting them finish in mid-air. There is no image in the physical gap between monitors, but the flight logic stays continuous. The maintenance entry points are `MainWindow.PairFeeding.cs` and this project's own `PairFeedFlight`, reusing the existing frames, props and transparent window with no new dependency.

2026-09-28, adopted shared daily rhythm: regular invitations begin about 12 seconds after coexistence is turned on, and are tried again 30-55 seconds after each scene ends. While temporarily busy it retries every 2 seconds and never force-interrupts a protected action. When either character's sleepiness reaches 70, napping takes priority; otherwise, if Nuonuo's hunger reaches 55 outside fasting and infinite modes, feeding takes priority. Ordinary play keeps alternating the cheek pinch and feeding with the ball mixed in, and when both are somewhat sleepy they may nap instead. Meeting, following and watching together are triggered separately by the [daily interaction rules](COMPANION_LIFE.md). After a successful end they stay where they finished for about 6 seconds before resuming autonomous activity, and a user action or a deletion event can cut that pause short. The right-click "Two-pet interactions" keeps its original entries and adds the four daily behaviours, and no scene starts while coexistence is off.

Approaching is calculated from physical distance and monitor DPI, taking about 0.85-5 seconds. Triggering and rhythm reuse the existing two-window coordination, and `PairInteractionPolicy` is only the shared pure rules for this project's invitations, relay and conversation, maintained by this project. When a scene ends it releases the coordination state before handling the next deletion in the queue, so the solo path is not entered by mistake while the previous scene is still finishing. Automated regressions cover the rules, the production entry points and the real anchors, while the desktop effect is accepted by the user.

Interactions that require approaching play the running animation in the direction actually travelled, and return to the interaction artwork's facing on arrival. A size change, a display layout change, locking or sleeping aborts the scene and cleans up the props; in the deletion relay an item not yet eaten returns to the queue, while an item already eaten does not re-enter it. Clicking the current size without the size actually changing does not interrupt the action.

## Two-pet relay for deleted icons

2026-09-29, adopted: after a hit, the fake icon is closed and counted only once. The coordinated scene keeps holding Nuonuo until the current `pair_feed_catch` swallow and chew finish (about 2.79 seconds after the action starts), then returns directly to idling or the next task. The solo `chomp` slurp animation must not cut in afterwards. A cancellation, drag or mode switch after a hit must also not rebuild an icon that has already been eaten. Automated tests call the production update and cancel entry points to check that playback continues after the ghost icon closes, and does not roll back.

Adopted: when Nuonuo receives a deletion event with an exact desktop position, both pets are present and both are available, Feibi Jiubi preferentially runs to the right of that icon and kicks it out while Nuonuo catches it in her open mouth on the other side. If the icon is at the left of the monitor and there is no room for Nuonuo on the left, the icon bounces off the left screen edge first and then into her mouth on the right. Both align to the same monitor's physical-pixel coordinates separately, and frame timing and running distance are calculated from the monitor dimensions. An icon at the top may drop slightly during the wind-up to a foot-contact height where it is fully visible. The fake icon and its text stay as a single in-place visual until the kick, at which point the text fades out and the icon flies on its own. The existing one-shot feeding count is called only when the mouth is hit, and the chew plays afterwards. Feibi gains no hunger value and no feeding count.

The real file is still deleted only by Windows. The relay reuses the existing deletion queue and the same single fake icon window, never generating a second icon, and batch deletions are handled one at a time. Feibi walking or being curious, and a window stored in her hat, no longer block the relay. While she is landing or waking, the current icon is retained and it waits up to 5 seconds; if she is asleep she is woken first, and the two set off together once ready. No new autonomous window attack starts during that wait. On a timeout, or while she is being dragged, pinched or attacking a window, or on a different monitor, or with no exact position, or genuinely without enough space, or with an invalid icon, it falls back to Nuonuo eating alone; the interrupted item and the original fake icon return to the head of the queue, with an 8-second cooldown to stop it waiting on the same one repeatedly. Quiet mode and turning deletion responses off abort the relay. `PairIconKickWaiting/Started/Impact/Ended/Skipped` record the queue number, the state and the fallback reason, and never the icon content; the old early-exit path no longer fails silently. The new coordination serves only this project's two-window interaction and is not abstracted into a general system.

Edge positioning uses the real foot and mouth markers and the character dimensions from the current official frames. The icon and its text stay in place while waiting and while they run over. Once both are in place and winding up, an icon at any of the four edges may slide slightly to a fully visible kicking position, so the first column of icons is not rejected merely because of transparent padding around the feet. Background tests cover the four corner positions on monitors with negative coordinates, at 60%/100%/200% body size and 100%/125%/150% DPI.

## Speech bubbles

Adopted: "Show speech bubbles" in the right-click menu is a shared toggle for both pets that persists across launches, and defaults to off where older settings lack the field. Once on, each character responds to some state changes with her own short lines, and says something occasionally while idle on her own. With both pets present and both available, they take turns showing one line each from their own dialogue, and right-clicking "Two-pet interactions → Let them chat" can request it deliberately. A deliberate request stops ordinary wandering and wakes a sleeping character, and waits up to 30 seconds while one of them is busy eating or making mischief. Each round is about 5.7 seconds, and both characters' autonomous actions are held during the talking; dragging, window mischief, an existing two-pet action and the safety states can all still interrupt. The bubble is its own window: transparent, passing the mouse through and never stealing focus, following the character and positioned by the monitor work area and DPI. Quiet mode hides the bubbles, and "Mute" affects sound only and does not change the text toggle. The test edition's entry point does not show this toggle, and bubbles do not play during the automated demonstration.

## Art and maintenance rationale

This round of daily-rhythm work and the deletion relay fix reuse the chosen assets below, with no image regenerated or iteratively generated; what changed is the invitation rules, the scene transitions and the real anchor positions. Two-pet conversation also allows ordinary walking to stop so a line can be answered, and a window in the hat no longer prevents talking. The speech-bubble toggle and the mute and quiet settings keep their original constraints.

Every character action sheet is generated directly from that character's **original image**, with no already-generated image or cropped frame fed back into the generation model. The prompts and rules for the new daily actions are in [two-pet daily interactions](COMPANION_LIFE.md):

- Feibi Jiubi's original: `assets/characters/feibijiubi/original.png`
- Nuonuo's original: `assets/characters/nuonuo/original.png`
- The six chosen action sheets generated straight from the originals: `assets/pair_interactions/sheets/`. Feeding uses `feed_throw_feibi.png` and `feed_catch_nuonuo_upright.png`; the earlier feeding drafts are kept as historical assets and are no longer used at runtime.
- The deterministic cropping script: `tools/process_pair_sprites.py`. It splits on the 16 complete character outlines in the source sheet, and does not cut off a hat top, hair or feet that cross an even grid line. Each action set is calibrated against the same character's neutral idle face width, and Feibi's throwing action after removing the hat is referenced to the final hat-removal frame. A whole set shares one scale factor and is positioned by the ground-contact foot, so it is not re-centred just because a reaching arm, hair tip or hat changed the bounding box.
- The 16-frame transparent runtime sequences: `runtime/pair_cheek`, `runtime/pair_sleep` and `runtime/pair_feed` for each character.
- Nuonuo's feeding frames are read at runtime from the same `runtime/pair_feed` folder in three beats — `pair_feed_wait` (frames 0-2, looping), `pair_feed_ready` (frames 3-5) and `pair_feed_catch` (frames 6-15) — with no image copied or regenerated; the food landing point is realigned to the new front-facing open-mouth frame.
- The complete hat removal and wearing reuse Feibi Jiubi's existing `runtime/hat_open` and `runtime/hat_wear`, and the new `pair_feed` frames cover only the reaching in and throwing once the hat is already off.
- Frame hashes and provenance record: `assets/pair_interactions/previews/pair-sprite-manifest.json`
- `assets/pair_interactions/calibration.json` stores the per-frame affine mapping of the annotated hand, hat-opening and mouth coordinates onto the new canvas. The official feeding, the deleted-icon relay and the offline previews all read it, so an old landing point is not used after the body size is calibrated. It serves only the two existing two-pet feeding paths and establishes no shared extension protocol.
- The icon-kick relay adds `icon_kick_feibi.png`, regenerated directly from Feibi's original and split into the 16 frames of `runtime/pair_icon_kick`; Nuonuo reuses the `pair_feed_wait/ready/catch` catching frames, likewise generated straight from her own original. The separate fake icon layer is not part of a generated action sheet.
- Generation inputs and action timelines: `docs/PAIR_SPRITE_PROMPTS.md`
- The four small food images are drawn by `tools/generate_pair_treats.py`, and are not a re-edit of a generated action sheet.

If the art needs adjusting later, go back to the original character image, regenerate the whole action sheet, then run the deterministic cropping script; do not use the current generated sheet as input to an image model. The two-pet interaction is only a local coordination layer over this project's existing `MainWindow` two-window architecture, and establishes no cross-project framework. The original solo icon eating and the real window mischief logic are not taken over by the interaction layer.

2026-09-28, adopted: after the two characters' ordinary walking and running were re-cropped from the originally chosen action sheets, they are registered with a deterministic translation based on the median horizontal position of the face, leaving the soles, the vertical stride and the original poses unchanged. The reason is that the old version centred on the whole outline, which produced extra horizontal jitter whenever hair tips or an extended leg changed the outline width. The implementation reuses the existing Pillow/OpenCV processing tools with no new dependency, and is maintained by this project. `tools/audit_motion_continuity.py` checks that the horizontal anchor range of a movement loop stays within 1.5 source pixels, and that the two-pet neutral pose's face width differs from idle by less than 10%; it does not count width changes from turning the head, crouching or stretching the face as a body-size fault.

## Previews and user testing

The offline asset previews for the cheek pinch and the nap use `tools/pair_interaction_demo.py`. The current feeding preview first exports the production `PairFeedFlight` and `SpriteAnimator` trajectories through the background logic test's `--feed-preview`, then `tools/remote_feed_preview.py` composes `dist/双宠互动演示/原地抛食-long.gif` and `原地抛食-reverse-height.gif` (the paths those tools create) to check long distance, left-right swapping and height differences respectively. The old fixed-position feeding preview no longer represents current behaviour. None of the previews start the pet, open a window or play sound; the real monitor DPI, window layering and manual interruption still need the user to confirm them.

Run the current source yourself, turn on "Both pets together", then right-click either character and work through the three items under "Two-pet interactions". Watch for whether the hand and the cheek actually touch during the pinch; whether the two lean together during the nap with no missing hat tops or feet; and during feeding, whether Feibi takes her hat off completely, whether the food appears from inside the hat and flies a visible distance, whether Nuonuo opens her mouth wide before it arrives, and whether Feibi puts her hat back on. Repeat the feeding to confirm all four random foods. Check separately in quiet mode, with a drag interruption, with coexistence turned off, and on monitors at different scale factors, that she returns to normal idling. If anything looks wrong, reporting the rough time range from the most recent diagnostic log under `%LOCALAPPDATA%/SoftMochiPet/` is enough to locate it.
