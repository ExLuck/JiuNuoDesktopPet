# Image generation input for two-pet interactions

The only reference images for the six action sheets below are `assets/characters/feibijiubi/original.png` and `assets/characters/nuonuo/original.png` respectively. The built-in imagegen produces a separate 4×4 action sheet directly from the matching original, and `tools/process_pair_sprites.py` then splits the frames deterministically. `assets/pair_interactions/previews/pair-sprite-manifest.json` records the chosen source image and the hash of every frame. Feeding a generated action sheet or a runtime frame back into the model is forbidden.

Constraints shared by all prompts: the 16 cells form a continuous timeline in row-major order; in every cell the character faces the viewer or turns slightly towards the interaction partner, keeping the same shot size and sole baseline, with the top of the head and both feet fully inside the frame; a single character, a transparent or pure white background, no second character, no scenery and no text. Feibi Jiubi must keep her large white hat with its black and blue trim, blonde hair, blue cross hairpin, purple eyes with highlights, white-black-blue uniform, black gloves and striped socks. Nuonuo must keep her long grey-blue hair, pupil-less two-tone pink and blue eyes, red and white clothes, red bow and both feet.

The new Nuonuo feeding sheet was regenerated in full by the built-in imagegen **using only `assets/characters/nuonuo/original.png` as reference**; neither the old `feed_catch_nuonuo.png` nor any generated frame was used as input. The prompt stresses a vertical head and stable soles for the first six frames, then the progressive mouth opening and puffed cheeks, in strict 4×4 row-major order on a transparent background with no second character, food or text. Feibi's throwing sheet likewise references only her own original artwork; the hat-off and hat-on frames reuse the existing runtime images and are not fed into the generation model. These prompts define the intent to generate, while the chosen images and their hashes define the actual production assets. If the image service is ever swapped out, regenerate completely from the character originals and the action specifications above, then check frame by frame for the head, hat, eyes, clothes, both feet, continuity between adjacent frames, and the sole anchor.

The icon-kick relay uses `icon_kick_feibi.png`: the built-in imagegen is given only `assets/characters/feibijiubi/original.png`, and is asked for Feibi in her complete hat kicking a small object towards screen left that is **not drawn**. The 4×4 timeline is excited anticipation (0-3), crouching wind-up (4-7), leg extended through the kick with contact held (8-11), and springing back to a pleased stance (12-15). Every cell keeps the hat, hair tips and both feet complete, with enough transparent margin around the small central figure; no icon, second character, grid lines or text. The first draft was discarded because the hair tips crossed a cell boundary, and the final sheet was regenerated completely from the same original, with no use of the discarded draft. The runtime icon and text come from the cache of the actual deletion target, not from the action sheet.
